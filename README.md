My submission for my ENRG 3310 Personal Energy Project: "Carrying the PyTorch Through the Night: Estimating Energy Usage of Image Classification with PyTorch"

I explored different methods of estimating energy usage of performing Image Classification using PyTorch, and extrapolating that usage in order to assess the environmental cost of running such involved computations compared to alternative behaviors.

Ultimately, it became clear the environmental costs of intensive overnight computations on a personal laptop, even if done several hundred times a year, are negligible in the scope of a lifestyle that includes driving an automobile.

My project earned the highest grade in the class for the Personal Energy Project.
